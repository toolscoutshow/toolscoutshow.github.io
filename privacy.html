Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Who we are

"Toolscout Studio" (also called "Toolscout Automation") is the private, internal application used by its owner to publish original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (@toolscoutshow) and TikTok account, and to read the performance statistics of those videos. See also our Terms of Service.

2. Use of YouTube API Services and TikTok APIs

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using this application, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The application also uses TikTok's Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API, subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Data we access

The application only accesses the owner's own accounts, with the owner's explicit authorization. It uses this access to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and their metadata (title, caption, description, tags) to those accounts;
  • read basic account information (display name, avatar) and the accounts' own video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time) to improve future videos.

The application does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other YouTube or TikTok user.

4. Storage and security

Authorization tokens and channel statistics are stored on a private server controlled by the channel owner, protected by access controls and encrypted connections. This data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

5. Retention and revocation

Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the channel. Access granted to the application can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Upon revocation, stored tokens become invalid and related data is deleted within 30 days.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools.
